STANDARD VENDOR AGREEMENT

This Vendor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of November 1, 2026, by and between CloudMatrix Technologies ("Vendor") and Apex Global Solutions Inc. ("Buyer").

SECTION 1. DELIVERABLES AND SERVICES
1.1 Software Subscriptions. Vendor shall grant Buyer access to its SaaS platform pursuant to standard service level commitments.
1.2 Support. Vendor will provide standard technical support during normal business hours.

SECTION 2. COMMERCIAL TERMS AND INVOICING
2.1 Payment Terms. Buyer shall remit all undisputed invoice payments within Net 30 days of receipt.
2.2 Reimbursable Costs. Pre-approved travel and out-of-pocket expenses shall be billed at cost without markup.
2.3 No Unreasonable Penalties. Neither party shall be assessed punitive fees or late charges exceeding 1.0% per month on delinquent sums.

SECTION 3. TERM AND TERMINATION
3.1 Fixed Term. This Agreement shall have a fixed term of twelve (12) months from the Effective Date. There shall be no automatic renewal; any extension requires a written mutual amendment.
3.2 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate upon thirty (30) days' written notice if the other party commits a material breach.
3.3 Termination for Convenience. Either party may terminate this agreement without cause upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice.

SECTION 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
4.1 Customer Ownership. Buyer retains all ownership rights in Customer Data.
4.2 Platform Ownership. Vendor retains all ownership in its pre-existing SaaS software and architecture.

SECTION 5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PRIVACY
5.1 Protection Period. Each party agrees to protect confidential information with reasonable care for a period of three (3) years following disclosure.
5.2 Data Privacy. Vendor shall process all personal data strictly in accordance with GDPR and applicable state privacy laws.

SECTION 6. MUTUAL LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
6.1 Aggregate Cap. Except for gross negligence or willful misconduct, each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall be capped at the total fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.
6.2 Exclusion of Consequential Damages. Neither party shall be liable for lost profits or indirect consequential damages.

SECTION 7. MUTUAL INDEMNIFICATION
7.1 Vendor IP Indemnity. Vendor shall defend and indemnify Buyer against third-party claims asserting that the SaaS service infringes a valid patent or copyright.
7.2 Buyer Indemnity. Buyer shall indemnify Vendor against third-party claims arising from unauthorized data provided by Buyer.

SECTION 8. AUDIT AND INSPECTION
8.1 Reasonable Notice. Vendor may inspect and audit usage logs upon at least thirty (30) business days' prior written notice during regular business hours.
